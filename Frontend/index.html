Richterbauer — Solar Self-Consumption Controller

Farm context

Richterbauer is a hay-fed dairy farm in Pinzgau, Salzburg, Austria. On-site photovoltaic (PV) generation, a small battery, and the public grid supply four main loads: milk cooling, milking robot, hay dryer, and barn ventilation. The controller maximizes solar self-consumption by scheduling flexible loads when surplus PV is available.
